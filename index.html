RFID-Based Automated Attendance Monitoring System

An Arduino-based system for CvSU-Bacoor City Campus that automatically detects RFID cards, shows attendance information on an LCD screen, and stores records digitally, replacing slow and error-prone manual attendance.

ArduinoRFIDLCD DisplayEmbedded SystemsSerial Communication

Read more

Attendance monitoring plays an important role in educational institutions because it helps track student participation, punctuality, and compliance with school policies. Traditional methods such as roll calls, attendance sheets, and logbooks often require significant time and effort from instructors, and may lead to misplaced records, inaccurate data, and delays in reporting, especially in large classes.

The proposed system uses Radio Frequency Identification (RFID), which enables fast and contactless identification through RFID cards and readers. It applies concepts of embedded systems, serial communication, and real-time processing through the integration of hardware and software components.

The system also supports automated attendance evaluation, real-time monitoring, and reliable data storage, giving students and instructors a practical and user-friendly solution.
